# How to put a client on the calendar

For Ron. Payments are manual: you decide how many sessions a client has, and you put them on your calendar yourself. Nothing here charges a card.

Two ideas to keep in mind:

- **A client's number of sessions is just a count you control.** You can book someone who has 0 or even a negative number (you will see "Owed 2"). Booking never depends on the count.
- **Nothing is taken off the count until you mark the session attended.** A session you put on the calendar is just a booking until then.

## 1. Add the client (once)

On the phone: tap **Home**, then **Add a client**. Or the **Clients** tab, then **Add client**.

1. Leave "A one-on-one client (their own space)" selected.
2. On **Add directly**, type their name. Their email is optional. Tap **Add client**.
3. Tap **Open their profile** when it offers it.

This makes the person a client of yours, in their own private space. They can sign in later. Use **Send a signup link** only when you want them to create their own login; it asks for their name first so their space is named properly.

## 2. Give them sessions (when they pay you)

On their profile, find **Assign sessions**. Type how many (for example 12), add a note if you like ("paid by Venmo Oct 6"), and tap **Assign sessions**. Their count goes up and the ledger on their profile records it.

If you just want to fix the number, use **Set balance** in the same box.

## 3. Put one session on the calendar

Phone: **Calendar** tab, pick the client at the top, tap the day, then **Assign** next to a time.
Desktop: drag the client's name onto a day, pick the time.

You can also open the client's profile and tap **Schedule session**. It opens the calendar with that client already picked.

Things you will see:

- A time that clashes with another session says **Busy**.
- If a session is at a time that is not one of your open slots (for example 6:30 AM, or a Sunday), it still shows up under **Other sessions today** with all its buttons.
- Times are shown in your time zone. If a time ever looks several hours off, open **Availability** once and check the time zone box at the top.

## 4. Put them on every week (repeating)

On the client's profile, find **Weekly schedule**. Pick the first date, the time and the length. Then choose:

- **Number of weeks** (1 to 52), or
- **No end date**. It keeps the next 12 weeks booked and tops itself up every morning. You can pause or end it any time.

You will see a preview of every date. Untick any date you want to skip (a holiday). Tap **Book N sessions**.

To change one week: open that day on the calendar and use **Cancel**. For a session that belongs to a weekly schedule, cancelling takes that one week off the schedule and it is not booked again.

## 5. After the session: mark it attended

Open that day on the calendar. Next to the session, tap **Mark attended**. One session comes off the client's count. If the count was already 0, it goes to **Owed 1**.

- **Undo** puts it back.
- **Don't charge** is for a holiday, a make-good or a freebie. That session never takes anything off.
- **No-show** records that they did not come. It does not charge by itself; use **Mark attended** if you want to charge it.

## 6. When someone has run out

On your Home (desktop) you will see **Needs payment**. For each client who is at 0 or owed:

- **Remind** sends them a gentle nudge (at most once every 3 days).
- **Hold** means "stop reminding this person" (comped, on a break, or pays another way). Use **Hold all** for a whole list at once, for example while everyone is still on Acuity.

A held client stays quiet until you take the hold off.

## 7. Cancel or move a session

- **Cancel**: open the day, tap **Cancel** next to the session. Anything charged for it is given back.
- **Move**: cancel it and book the new time (there is no one-tap move for a single session yet). For a weekly schedule, use the schedule on the client's profile to change "this and the rest".
