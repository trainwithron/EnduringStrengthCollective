# Real-phone check of the email links (about 5 minutes, after this build is deployed)

Why: the sign-up confirmation, the coach invite and the reset email used to land on "Link invalid or expired" (the page could not read the link). The pages now read the link themselves. Only a real phone and a real inbox can prove it.

**Before you start (2 minutes, in the dashboards):** (a) Vercel, the project's environment variables: NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL is set to the real site address (for example https://your-site.vercel.app, no trailing slash). (b) Supabase, Authentication, URL Configuration: the Site URL is that same address, and the Redirect URLs list contains that address's /set-password and /confirm-email (or just that address followed by /**). It must NOT contain a wildcard for every site such as *.vercel.app.

1. You need an email inbox you can open on your phone. On the phone, open the live site in the browser, tap Sign up, and make a throwaway coach with that email. Open the confirmation email on the phone and tap the link. PASS: you land in the app (Home), signed in. FAIL: a screen saying "Link invalid or expired" (screenshot it and send it to Spot).
2. On a computer, open the site, on the sign-in page tap "Forgot password?" (under the password box), enter that same email. Open the reset email on your PHONE and tap the link. PASS: a "Set your password" page shows with "Setting a password for <your email>" (before, a link asked for on one device failed on another). Set a password and confirm you land in the app. FAIL: "Link invalid or expired", or no email within 5 minutes (check spam; send Spot a screenshot and the time).
3. Sign in as yourself on the computer. Open a test client whose email is also yours (a client who has signed in), Overview tab, press "Email them a sign-in link". PASS: it says "Sent to x•••@..." and the email arrives; opening it on the phone shows the set-password page. If it says the email was changed in the last 24 hours, or is not confirmed, that is the safety rule working: use a different test client.
4. If a "This link is for a different account" screen ever appears, that is also correct: it means you were signed in as someone else; pick "Use this link" only if you asked for it.
5. Delete the throwaway coach afterwards (tell Spot; no real data was made).
