# Exercise Library — Verified Demo Video Links

Compiled via live web search and direct verification (YouTube oEmbed / channel checks) on September 8, 2026. Every link below was checked to confirm it is a real, currently-loading video published by the stated channel — no URLs were guessed or pattern-generated. That said, video platforms change over time (videos get taken down, re-titled, or made private), so **please spot-check links before publishing them to clients**, especially a few months from now.

Sources used, in priority order:
1. **OPEX Fitness** — their official "Exercise Library" series on YouTube (channel: OPEX Fitness).
2. **RP Strength** — Renaissance Periodization's YouTube channel (channel: Renaissance Periodization / Dr. Mike Israetel), mainly their "Targeting The Muscle" technique series.
3. **Fallback sources** — clearly labeled "Fallback —" whenever OPEX/RP didn't have a good demo for that exact exercise. These are reputable, well-established strength/coaching channels (e.g., Catalyst Athletics, Horton Barbell, Concept2/ErgFit, WODprep), but they are a step down in priority from the two requested sources.

Where OPEX only had a close variant of an exercise (not the exact standard name), that's noted next to the link so you know what you're actually getting.

### Notes for the coach

- Every URL above was individually confirmed to be a real, loading YouTube video, and the publishing channel was checked (via YouTube's own oEmbed metadata) to make sure "OPEX Exercise Library"-titled videos actually belong to OPEX Fitness and "Targeting The Muscle" videos actually belong to Renaissance Periodization — a handful of look-alike titles from other channels (e.g., "Horton Barbell," a channel that also uses the phrase "(Exercise Library)" in its titles) were caught and excluded from the OPEX group, then re-labeled as fallback where they were still a good demo.
- A few OPEX links are the closest available **variant** rather than the exact plain-vanilla version of the movement (e.g., banded lat pulldown instead of a stack-machine pulldown, kettlebell sumo deadlift instead of barbell) — this is flagged inline everywhere it applies, since OPEX's own programming leans on bands/kettlebells/bodyweight over machines.
- Because RP Strength's channel is bodybuilding/hypertrophy-focused, it has essentially no coverage of Olympic lift variations (cleans, snatches), sled work, kettlebell swings, or carries — those categories rely on OPEX plus fallback sources instead.
- Re-verify links roughly every few months, or before a big publish push — creators occasionally delete, privatize, or re-upload videos under new IDs.
